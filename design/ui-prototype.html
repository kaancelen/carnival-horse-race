KEY UX DECISIONS

  • Thumb law: the bottom 35% of the screen belongs to the player alone. Never place a button, ad or toast there.
  • Two distinct scores: "points" (earned from holes) and "distance" (horse position) must never look alike — points on the bar, distance on the track.
  • Rival visibility: show only the lead rival in the HUD, not all four. The rest is noise.
  • The losing beat: delay the defeat screen by 800ms so the player watches on the track why they lost.
  • Android back button: defined on every screen — Pause during a race, exit confirmation in the menu.

TABLET ADAPTATION

  • Portrait stays locked; at 4:3 the track and board keep their ratio and the sides fill with tent texture.
  • On tablet the throw zone isn't pinned to the bottom edge — it anchors to the last touch point, so one-handed play still reaches it.
  • Type doesn't scale unbounded: clamp it with min(vw*0.06, 64px) logic.

ACCESSIBILITY

  • Holes are never distinguished by colour alone: size plus the printed number carry the same information. Critical for colour blindness.
  • Minimum touch target 44×44 pt; the holes are already 90/120/150 px on the 1080 canvas.
  • Text contrast: dark brown on cream = 11:1. Deep ink on gold = 8.5:1.
  • Respect the system "reduce motion" setting: confetti and screen shake switch off.

  • Every colour group totals 6 points. Size makes the risk/reward symmetry visible without a word of explanation: smaller means worth more.

MOTION & FEEDBACK

  • Ball throw: 350 ms arc, ease-out-quad. In Godot: Tween.TRANS_QUAD, EASE_OUT.
  • Hole hit: 120 ms scale 1→1.22→1 plus a bulb flare and a short haptic tap.
  • Score popup: "+6" rises 24 px out of the hole and fades over 600 ms.
  • Horse advance: 600 ms ease-out-back — the slight overshoot is what makes it feel like momentum.
  • Screen shake: red hits only, 4 px / 100 ms. Any more than that becomes fatiguing fast.
  • Audio: hoofbeat tempo tracks the player's speed; crowd noise swells as the gap narrows. (AudioDirector is already written for exactly this.)

PORTING TO GODOT

  • Keep every colour in one place — res://ui/theme/palette.gd as const values — and derive the .tres theme from those.
  • Button and card styles should be 9-slice StyleBoxTexture, not StyleBoxFlat. Wood grain and cream paper cannot be faked with a gradient.
  • The main Theme resource needs all four states — normal / hover / pressed / disabled — defined for Button, Label, Panel and PanelContainer.
  • stretch/mode=canvas_items is already correct; add aspect=expand so tablets get side padding instead of stretched art.
  • Blinking bulbs are an AnimationPlayer + modulate job, not a shader — free on mobile GPUs.
  • Update the StyleBoxFlat colours already in ScoringBoard.tscn to this palette: red #C8352C, yellow #F5B942, green #46A055.
